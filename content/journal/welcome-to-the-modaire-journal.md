The Modaire Journal is where we'll be sharing everything the marketplace can't quite fit into a listing page — buyer guides, seller stories, styling notes, and the small decisions that add up to a more sustainable modest wardrobe.

## What you'll find here

We built Modaire to make it easier to buy and sell **preloved modest fashion** — abayas, kaftans, hijabs, Pakistani bridal wear, shalwar kameez, and everything in between. The marketplace itself does the transactional work. The Journal is the slower conversation around it.

Expect:

- **Buyer guides** — how to shop for preloved bridal wear, size a vintage abaya you can't try on, or evaluate the condition of a listed piece from photos alone.
- **Seller spotlights** — profiles of the sellers who make Modaire what it is, from small-batch designers cleaning out sample lots to individual sellers with one perfect kaftan to pass on.
- **Styling notes** — how to layer a modest western look, style a saree for a wedding you're a guest at, or find a hijab that finally holds a pin.
- **Sustainability & value** — why buying preloved matters for the modest wear space specifically, and how to build a wardrobe that lasts.

## Why "preloved" and not "used"

The clothes on Modaire are usually barely-worn — bridal outfits from a single wedding, formal pieces from one dinner, everyday clothes retired because a size didn't quite work. Calling them "used" undersells what they are. Preloved captures that these are pieces someone chose, wore, and passed on carefully.

That's the tone of the Journal too. Care, context, and useful recommendations — no marketing, no clickbait, no listicles built to game a search engine.

Thanks for being here.
